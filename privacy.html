Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how Alpen Health (“we”, “us”) handles personal data collected through www.alpen-health.com.

Data we collect

When you submit the partnership enquiry form or write to us, we receive the information you choose to share: your name, company, position, business email address, market and therapeutic area of interest and the content of your message.

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking on this website. The contact page displays an embedded OpenStreetMap map, which is loaded from openstreetmap.org.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange follow-up discussions.
  • To keep a record of business correspondence.

We do not sell your data or use it for unrelated marketing.

Confidential information

Please do not send confidential commercial, product or patient information through the website form. Confidential exchanges can take place once an appropriate agreement is in place.

Retention

Enquiry data is kept only as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any resulting business relationship, then deleted.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data at any time by writing to info@alpen-health.com.
