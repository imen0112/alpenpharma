Therapeutic areas

Where therapeutic expertise meets market execution.

Different therapies require different stakeholders, access pathways and commercial models.

Our approach

The right strategy for each therapy.

Alpen Health combines therapeutic understanding with deep local market expertise to build the right strategy, engagement model and path to growth for each opportunity.

For every area, we start from the same questions: who decides, how the therapy is funded and purchased, and what it takes to reach patients reliably.

01

Immunology, Plasma & Rare Diseases

Complex therapies. Specialist ecosystems. Critical access.

Small patient populations, a limited number of expert centres and institutional purchasing. Success depends on reaching the right specialists, securing access pathways and protecting continuity of supply.

  • Expert centres
  • Hospital pharmacy
  • Institutional procurement

Women’s Health, Fertility & Reproductive Medicine

Specialist science. Patient-centred pathways.

Care is led by gynaecologists and fertility specialists, often in private clinics, where scientific credibility and the patient experience shape adoption.

Ophthalmology & Vision Care

Precision. Specialist engagement. Long-term value.

A specialist-driven field where adoption follows clinical evidence, hands-on training and long-term relationships with ophthalmologists and surgical centres.

  • Ophthalmologists
  • Eye surgery centres
  • Hospitals & clinics

Oncology & Supportive Care

Specialised science. Complex pathways. Meaningful access.

Multidisciplinary decisions, high-value therapies and structured funding make market access and medical engagement as decisive as the product itself.

  • Payers & tenders

Neurology & Specialty Therapies
