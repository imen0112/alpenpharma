# Benchmark — commercialisation partners in specialty pharma

Companies with a model close to Alpen Health's (a commercialisation partner for international pharma companies in emerging regions), reviewed from what their public websites show.

| Company | Base / region | What their site does well |
|---|---|---|
| **Swixx BioPharma** | Switzerland → Central & Eastern Europe | One clear promise to partners, a market map with each country, leadership shown with faces and full bios, compliance and safety reporting easy to find |
| **Ewopharma** | Switzerland → CEE | Restrained Swiss design, a strong "partner of choice" message, a short partnership process, legal and pharmacovigilance pages that feel credible |
| **NewBridge Pharmaceuticals** | Dubai → Middle East & Africa | Therapeutic areas shown as a structured list, a dedicated partners page, an institutional tone with no hype |
| **Biologix** | Dubai → MENA | Specialty and rare-disease positioning, country pages, a "why partner with us" section |
| **DKSH Healthcare** | Zurich → Asia | Market-expansion services shown as a value chain from strategy to execution |

## What they have in common, and what we applied

1. **The partner, not the patient, is the main reader.** The pages speak to business-development heads at pharma companies. Applied in the copy, in the "You bring / Alpen brings / Together we build" model and in CTAs that ask for a partnership discussion.
2. **The capabilities form a chain.** Strategy, then regulatory, market access, medical and commercial. Applied as six numbered capabilities and the Assess → Sustain operating model, drawn as an altitude profile.
3. **Each country gets its own page or block**, with a role that differs from one country to the next. Applied on the Markets page (Tunisia as the platform, Algeria for strategic development, Libya for selective opportunities).
4. **Credibility comes from restraint.** No made-up figures, real photography, thin rules, plenty of white space, legal and privacy pages, real contact details and a real address with a map.
5. **Leadership is shown by name.** All of these sites show their leaders. Still to do: add photos, names and short bios (the slot is ready on the About page, `#leadership`).
6. **Safety and compliance are easy to reach.** Most of them link a pharmacovigilance or adverse-event contact from the footer. Recommended: confirm which address handles safety reporting (for example medical@) and add it.
